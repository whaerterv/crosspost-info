Crosspost

Crosspost is a personal desktop tool. It uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

Privacy policy

Crosspost runs only on the owner's computer. It has no server and no other users.

It uses YouTube access only to upload videos the owner chooses and to read the name of the owner's channel. Login tokens stay on the owner's computer. No data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions.

Use of YouTube features is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
